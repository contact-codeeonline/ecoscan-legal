# EcoScan Privacy Policy
_Last updated: October 26, 2026_

EcoScan ("we") does not require you to create an account. This policy explains what data the app processes.

## Data we collect
- **Display name** you enter during onboarding.
- **Randomly generated participant ID** created on first launch. It is not tied to your Apple ID, email or phone number.
- **Product photos or product URLs** you submit for analysis.
- **Scan history**: analysis results and timestamps linked to your participant ID.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifier, or track you across other apps or websites.

## How we use it
- To analyze products and show results to you.
- [For research purposes: aggregated, anonymized scan data may be used in an academic study.]

## Third-party processors
- **Supabase** – database and file storage. https://supabase.com/privacy
- **OpenAI** – AI analysis of submitted images/URLs. Per OpenAI's API policy, API data is not used to train models. https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy

We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

## Retention & deletion
Data is kept [for the duration of the study / up to 12 months] and then deleted.
To delete your data at any time, email **[your-email]** with your participant ID (shown in [Settings]). We will delete it within 30 days.

## Children
EcoScan is not directed to children under 13.
