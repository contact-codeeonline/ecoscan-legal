# EcoScan User Guide

EcoScan uses AI to assess how environmentally friendly a consumer product is, and how trustworthy its "green" claims are.

## 1. Getting started
1. Open EcoScan for the first time.
2. Enter your **display name** on the welcome screen.
3. EcoScan creates a **participant ID** for you automatically. No account, email or password is needed.

## 2. Scan a product
You can analyze a product in two ways:

**By photo**
1. Choose to scan by image.
2. Take a photo or pick one from your library (max 10 MB).
3. Make sure the label, packaging or any eco claims ("recyclable", "eco-friendly", certification logos…) are clear and readable.

**By product link**
1. Choose to scan by URL.
2. Paste the link to a product page from an online store.

Then submit and wait a few seconds for the result.

## 3. Understanding the result
- **Green Score (0–100)**: higher means the product looks more environmentally friendly, or its eco claims are better supported.
- **Risk level**: *Low*, *Medium* or *High*. This is the risk that the product's eco claims are vague, unsupported or misleading (greenwashing).
- **Explanation**: why the product received this score.
- **Warnings**: specific points to watch out for, such as missing evidence or vague wording.

If EcoScan cannot analyze the input, you may see:
- **Insufficient information**: the photo is unclear, or the product details are not visible. Try a sharper, closer photo of the label.
- **Invalid input**: the photo or link does not show a consumer product.

## 4. Tips for better results
- Photograph the label and packaging in good light, without glare.
- Include eco claims, certification logos and material information in the frame.
- Use one product per photo.

## 5. Important note
Results are generated by AI from what is visible in the photo or on the product page. They are for reference and learning purposes only, not an official certification. Always check important information with the manufacturer.
